About

Five years of turning complexity into clarity.

I've spent the past five years working as a digital creative, with most of that experience built at ATMOsphere — contributing across graphic design, web, video, print, photography and 3D. Alongside that, I've spent over fifteen years behind the camera, which still shapes how I see composition, light and storytelling in everything I make. Working in an international, technical environment taught me how to make complex subjects clearer, more visual, and more accessible.

I'm naturally curious and tech-oriented: I like discovering new tools, experimenting with different approaches, and understanding the systems behind the work — whether that's design, code, or content. My approach is both creative and structured, with a strong sensitivity to visual consistency, clarity of messaging and quality of execution.

Today I'm looking to bring that mix of curiosity and craft to a new environment — one where the work has a tangible impact and a clear vision behind it.
