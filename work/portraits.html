Faces
found
in light

Photography — portraits 2016—2021

An ongoing collection of environmental portraits — friends and collaborators caught wherever the light happens to be: neon signage, fairground bulbs, forest dusk, a passing flash of colour.

01
Approach

Mostly handheld, often after dark — letting whatever light is already there (streetlights, signage, sunsets, gels) shape the frame.

02
Subject

Friends, collaborators and chance moments — fairgrounds, forests, city streets and rooftops at dusk.

03
Equipment

Digital — Nikon 800D, Sony Alpha A7R II. Film — Olympus Trip XB400, Nikon F801s, Mamiya RZ67.

01 Gallery
In passing light

Light
finds
the
face.

An ongoing collection shot between 2016 and 2021 — portraits of friends and collaborators, made wherever the light happened to fall.

No studio, no setups — just neon, sunsets, fairground bulbs and forest dusk, and whoever was standing there at the time.
