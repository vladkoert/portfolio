Nuvo

A set of event posters and Instagram content for Nuvo, a nightclub in Kinshasa — built around a green-and-red duotone system for the venue's "Kulturr" night series, with a recurring wireframe-orb motif and a bold, all-caps voice.

Nuvo asked for a flexible poster system for its recurring Kulturr nights — something that could carry a lineup of DJs and artists one week, and a single mood-driven teaser the next.

A grungy black base, a wireframe orb and a strict red-or-green duotone hold the series together across stills and video — red leads, as the club's signature colour.
