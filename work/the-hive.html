Branding — visual identity 2025 Coworking

the hive

A visual identity for the hive, a coworking space — a hexagonal mark where the letter "h" converges at the centre to form an entrance, paired with Aktiv Grotesk and a single, confident blue.

01 Identity
One mark, one colour

A
house,
a
hexagon.

The hive needed a mark that could work as small as a favicon and as large as a sign above the door — a single hexagonal cell with an "h" built into its frame, doubling as a doorway.

Aktiv Grotesk and one saturated blue keep the system calm and consistent across every touchpoint.
