Laboriver

A full card system for Laboriver, an analogue photo lab in Brussels — eight prepaid process cards, each colour-coded to a different combination of develop, scan and proof, plus a fidelity card, gift cards, vouchers, a starter pack and a sheet of stickers. One process, one colour, one card.

A card

Laboriver asked for a small card system that would make their counter feel like part of the brand — prepaid cards for every combination of develop, scan and proof, each one carrying its own colour.

Fidelity, gift and referral cards round out the set, all built around the same outlined wordmark and a warm, film-roll motif.
